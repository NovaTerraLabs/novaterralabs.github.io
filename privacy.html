Privacy Policy

Fidget Toys: Relax & ASMR · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use the mobile game Fidget Toys: Relax & ASMR (the "App"). If you have any question, contact us at novaterrlaabs0@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. The App stores a few settings on your device only (sound and vibration on/off, which toys are unlocked, and how many days in a row you opened the App). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Who the App is for

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13.

Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. An ad is only shown when you choose to watch one (for example, to unlock a toy); you never have to watch ads to play the other toys. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and older.

AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where allowed and where you have agreed, this information may be used to show ads that are more relevant to you (personalised ads). How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads).

Your choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can refuse. If you are in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. These choices are shown when you first open the App, and you can change them at any time from the App's Settings ("Privacy Options").

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is processed about you, to have it deleted, or to object to its processing. Since we do not keep personal data ourselves, you can exercise these rights with Google directly, or contact us and we will help.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App has no accounts, no chat and no social features. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us at novaterrlaabs0@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We do not run our own servers and do not store any of your personal data. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own retention and security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date at the top.
